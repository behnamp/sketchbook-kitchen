Forty cookies. One scale. No guesswork.

From brown butter chocolate chunk to saffron cardamom butter cookies, this sketchbook-style collection gathers forty cookies for every mood: the classics, thick bakery-style giants, bright fruit and citrus, warm spice and nut, and wholesome gluten-free and vegan bakes.

Every recipe is written in grams and comes with the numbers that matter at a glance: batch size, dough-ball weight, oven temperature, bake time and chill time. Each one sits beside a hand-drawn watercolor illustration.
