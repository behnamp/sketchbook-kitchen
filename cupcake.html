Forty cupcakes. One scale. No guesswork.

From vanilla bean and red velvet to pistachio rose and saffron cardamom, this sketchbook-style collection gathers forty cupcakes for every occasion: the classics, deep chocolate, bright fruit and citrus, warm spice and nut, and showstoppers for celebrations.

Every recipe is written in grams and comes with the numbers that matter at a glance: batch size, batter per liner, oven temperature and bake time, each with its own frosting and a hand-drawn watercolor illustration.

Inside: a frosting and piping guide, a conversion chart, a troubleshooting page, and room for your own recipe.
